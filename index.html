Privacy Policy

App: curlythinks-poster. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

This policy covers the app "curlythinks-poster" ("the app"). The app is a private publishing tool. Its only job is to upload videos and captions to Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that belong to the app's own operator.

Who can use the app

The app is not offered to the public. It can only be used by its operator and by people the operator has added to the app. Nobody else can log in to it.

What data the app handles

The app uses access tokens issued by Meta so that it can post to the operator's own Pages and accounts. It reads the list of those Pages and basic information about the posts it has published. It does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, messages, location or any other personal data from members of the public, from viewers, or from followers.

How data is stored

Access tokens are kept on the operator's own computer and are not shared with anyone. The app has no database of user data, uses no cookies, shows no advertising and does no tracking or profiling.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Videos and captions are sent to Meta only in order to publish them.

Deleting data

Because the app holds no personal data about other people, there is nothing to delete for them. The operator can remove the app's access at any time in Facebook settings under "Business integrations", which makes the stored tokens useless. Anyone with a question or a deletion request can send a message to any Facebook Page the app publishes to, and it will be answered there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
